How a safe deal works

1

Agree in the chat

Make an offer with "Make a safe deal". Both sides sign a simple agreement.

2

Buyer pays Amanat

The money is held, not sent to the seller. Nobody can touch it without a person approving.

3

Meet or ship

Meet at a safe spot with selfie and GPS proof, or ship by courier with tracking.

4

Release code

Happy with the item? Give the seller your 4-digit code and they get paid.

Built against scams

Verified people

Phone, NID and two live selfies. Payouts only go to an account in the same name as the NID.

Scam warnings

The chat warns you when someone asks for an OTP or wants to be paid outside the app.

Fair disputes

Both sides send evidence. The AI suggests refund, release or split, and a person decides.

SOS and trip sharing
